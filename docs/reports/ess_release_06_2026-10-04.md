## Source and interpretation

(algorithm table), plus hardware manual physical12, were reviewed. These are
ordinary reviewed RO fields, not actions/read-to-clear fields. Bit4 set means
released; clear means enabled. Named alarm0,1,2,3,5 decode; raw4 remains an unnamed
error. Unknown alarms and status bits survive. Code4 or an asserted alarm flag

flags alone establish axis readiness.

in subdivision equivalents in the reviewed closed-loop mode. It never supplies
raw encoder counts. Configured4000 counts do not identify or measure an encoder.

source stays unresolved. The table does not establish speed units/signed encoding
or position signed encoding/physical scale. Raw unsigned bits are retained with
explicit reasons; no fabricated RPM, angle, actual position or completion.

## APIs, lifetime and freshness

`prepareState` copies exact target/id/address/generation, operation, absolute
deadline, host tuple and optional matching configuration's operation/order/
algorithm. No caller pointer survives. `nextRead` yields bounded copied work;
`advanceRead` copies retained frame evidence. `getStateBlock` publishes each
successful block even when a later block fails; absent/failed output is unchanged.
The three blocks are independent, not an atomic drive snapshot.

Application cache admission records last attempt. A successful block keeps the
actual Runner request-start as a conservative observation lower bound including
bus wait; qualified closure is its upper bound/last success and delivery remains
separate. Age is now minus lower bound. Public step `attemptedUs` is eligibility,
not physical TX/sample time. The drive may internally cache registers; its exact
sample age is undocumented. Failed refreshes retain previous values/times,
newer evidence/generations resist old delivery, repeated queries do not alter age,
and recovery makes old history non-current. All blocks use5000ms freshness here.

Communication has its own target/generation/lower/upper/age, including checked
exceptions; model-probe `age_ms` labels its own source. State, alarms, unknown
readiness and active-operation outcomes remain separate. Identity/configuration
reads cannot refresh state or resolve write uncertainty.

One cooperative owner task/UART remains. Eight foreground records plus a separate
recovery result are retained until explicit release. One private polling record
has an explicit cache consumer/release and cannot be inspected as a user result.
Owner normal pending/results4/8 and reserved urgent1/1 remain unchanged. Polling
is off at boot, bounded100..60000ms and1..1000 attempts, no catch-up burst. Rejected
admission consumes one attempt; normal priority lets urgent work run first.
`monitor off` cancels only local work and settles physical TX; recovery disables
polling/old continuations. No new task, queue, replay, retries or motor stop.
